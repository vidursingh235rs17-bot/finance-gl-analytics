Key findings FY2025-26

  • Revenue grew 13.1% to ₹96.41 Cr, 0.6% short of budget.
  • EBITDA fell 13.5% to ₹7.15 Cr, ₹3.05 Cr below budget.
  • Gross margin dropped from 28.9% to 26.7%: Bearings grew fastest but earns the lowest margin.
  • Freight overran budget by ₹85 L and professional fees by ₹54 L.
